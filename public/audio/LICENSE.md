# SUDUEL original sound bank

All WAV files in this directory are original synthesized sound effects authored
for SUDUEL by `scripts/generate-audio.mjs`. No external recordings, samples,
copyrighted melodies, sound libraries, or temporary audio URLs are used.

These assets are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0, to the extent
permitted by law. You may use, modify, distribute and commercially ship them.
See https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ for the legal instrument.

Reproduce the exact bank with `node scripts/generate-audio.mjs`. The generated
manifest records each cue's duration and byte length. These are the shipped
arcade cues, not placeholders requiring a third-party audio service.
